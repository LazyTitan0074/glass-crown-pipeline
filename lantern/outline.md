# Book Summary
The Lantern Room: a closed-circle mystery. A lighthouse off the Maine coast, winter 1923. Four people stranded by a four-day storm: Ruth (36, keeper), Elias (29, her brother), a guest with a locked valise, and Jonah (18, the assistant). One of the four is not who they claim. The reveal lands in chapter 4 and must be consistent with facts planted in chapter 1.

# Chapter List
4 chapters

# Chapter Details

## Chapter 1: First Night
### Summary
The storm's first night. Ruth logs the lamp while Elias boards the gallery door. A half-drowned guest woman reaches the rocks with a locked valise, weighed at eleven pounds and stored unopened. She gives no name. Jonah works silently and speaks no line.
### Key Events
- The guest arrives with the locked eleven-pound valise, name withheld.
- Elias and Ruth established plainly as brother and sister.
- Radio warns of four days of storm; Jonah seen but silent.
- Lights out with the lamp turning.

## Chapter 2: Names
### Summary
Day two. The guest introduces herself: Margaret, 44. Jonah speaks his first on-page line about the lamp oil. The radio dies into static. Elias inventories the four rooms; Margaret asks after her locked valise.
### Key Events
- Margaret named at last; Jonah speaks.
- The radio fails mid-broadcast.
- The valise confirmed locked and untouched.
- Sibling accounts with no warmth beyond family.

## Chapter 3: Doubt
### Summary
Day three. Elias accuses the storm, the guest, himself; Ruth holds the sibling line. Jonah repeats something Margaret said that does not match her story — suspicion with no named target. Ruth weighs the valise at eleven pounds and puts it back unopened.
### Key Events
- Jonah's mismatch plants doubt without naming the liar.
- Ruth and Elias hold as brother and sister only.
- The valise re-anchored: eleven pounds, unopened.
- The storm does not lift.

## Chapter 4: The Lantern Room
### Summary
Day four. Cornered by Ruth's questions, the liar is revealed — one of the four is not who they claim, proven by chapter-1 facts. The valise is opened at last and settles it. The storm ends after.
### Key Events
- The reveal, earned by planted facts.
- The valise opened in chapter 4, never before.
- Nobody left the lighthouse before this chapter.
- The lamp keeps turning for whoever keeps it next.
