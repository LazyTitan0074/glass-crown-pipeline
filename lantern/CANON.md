# CANON — The Lantern Room (cold-run test canon, guards front-loaded)
Authoritative fact checklist. ANY contradiction in a chapter => chapter REJECTED, no smoothing.

CHECKER RULES (anti-false-positive, binding): only DECLARED facts count
(names / ages / filiation / events / numbers / chronological appearances).
Behaviour, voice, or implied mood is NEVER a violation.
A quote that CONFIRMS a fact is NEVER a violation.

## People
- C01: STATED AGE 36 — Ruth is exactly 36, keeper of the lighthouse. Never write her younger/older.
- C02: Elias is Ruth's YOUNGER brother by exactly seven years (29). Sibling bond ONLY — NEVER romantic, NEVER sensual language between them, no lingering gazes, no warmth framed as attraction. Familial care is CORRECT, not a violation.
- C03: STAGING: Margaret arrives in chapter 1, but her full name is NOT spoken until chapter 2. Ch1: "the guest", "the woman", "her" ONLY. Writing "Margaret said" in ch1 IS a real violation.
- C05: STAGING: Jonah does not SPEAK on-page until chapter 2. He may be MENTIONED/described in ch1 (seen coiling rope, a silent figure), but ZERO dialogue lines and ZERO dialogue tags ("Jonah said", "he muttered") in ch1. Any ch1 Jonah dialogue IS a real violation.
- C10: STATED AGE 18 — Jonah is exactly 18, the assistant. Never write him younger/older.
- C08: Exactly one of the four is not who they claim. The reveal lands ONLY in chapter 4. Ch1–3 must contain NO sentence naming or implying who it is ("Margaret is not who she claims" in ch3 IS a real violation). Dread and suspicion without a target are CORRECT.

## Object

## Locked objects (FIX-3: contents are canon too)
- L01: Margaret's valise contains EXACTLY: a bundle of receipts, a brass key, a sealed tin canister. Contents may be revealed progressively (fewer items shown early) but never replaced: any other item stated to be inside (a photograph, letters, or anything else) IS a contents-drift violation.

## World

- C07: The storm lasts exactly FOUR days total and ends AFTER chapter 4. Clear skies before the end of ch4 IS a real violation.
- C09: NO character leaves the lighthouse before chapter 4. Stepping onto the gallery/landing for air is allowed (still the lighthouse); taking a boat, reaching the mainland, or being rescued before ch4 IS a real violation.
