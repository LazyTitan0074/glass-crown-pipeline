# Publishing adult fiction (practical notes)

## Where (2026 landscape)
- **AO3** — free, best-in-class tagging + content warnings, noncommercial. Ideal first home for 18+ work.
- **itch.io** — pay-what-you-want, ~90% to author, no content friction. Pair with AO3.
- **Smashwords / Draft2Digital** — free, distribute to Apple/Kobo/Barnes; erotica allowed with adult filters.
- **Amazon KDP** — biggest market, 35–70% royalties, but erotica is filtered from search/recommendations and policy enforcement is strike-based. Consensual-adult content is allowed; keep public descriptions clean, warnings inside.
- **Patreon / Ko-fi** — subscriptions for serialized work, adult settings on.
- **Own blog (WordPress)** — full control, zero starting traffic.

## Rules that travel with this pipeline
- 18+ labeling is mandatory, everywhere. Content warnings up front (see blurb template below).
- **Minors: never in intimate scenes, never described sensually, never subject to sexualized language.** This is a load-bearing rule of the system — any book built with it inherits the obligation.
- Use a pen name + separate email for publishing accounts.

## Blurb template (adapt freely)
> **[Title]** — [logline, 1–2 sentences]. [Stakes + choice, ~100 words.] Dark [genre]. [Open/closed] ending. [Word count].
>
> **Content warnings (18+ — ADULTS ONLY):** [list everything explicitly: kink dynamics, settings, violence, death themes — always consensual, never coerced]
>
> **Safety note:** [state plainly what is NOT in the book: no minors in sexual contexts, no non-consensual content, etc.]

## Legal (not legal advice)
Consensual-adult fiction is legal in RO/EU/US. AI-generated covers are allowed on KDP but must be disclosed where asked. Stock art needs commercial licenses (Unsplash/Pexels OK). If in doubt about a scene, cut it — no book is worth a policy strike.
